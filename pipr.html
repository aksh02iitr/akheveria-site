01

Context

Pipr is a Shopify store's AI teammate. A copilot you can ask anything about the business, which spots what to fix and drafts the work. And a help desk that answers every DM on Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook from the live store, and sells while it does. I lead product and design, build the frontend and make the launch films.

02

The problem

A D2C founder runs the store across Shopify, three DM inboxes, a WhatsApp tool and a spreadsheet. Hundreds of chats a day, most at night when the team is offline. The best signal about what sells is buried in those chats, and dashboards only show what already happened.

03

Research

How

Calls with D2C foundersReal DM threads from live storesTeardown of chatbot builders and AI help desksShopify, Meta and WhatsApp API limits

What we learned

Insight 01

Support chats sell

Over half of sales for brands on Pipr close inside conversations.

Insight 02

The night shift

Shoppers message most after the team logs off.

Insight 03

Answers, not dashboards

Founders ask in plain words: what sold, who stopped buying, why refunds are up.

Insight 04

Trust is the product

Nobody lets AI send a campaign or edit a listing without seeing it first.

05

Design principles

01

Chat-first

The home screen is the agent. Every other screen shows what it did.

02

Confirm before act

Nothing is sent, listed or charged without a click on a card.

03

Connect, then reveal

No hollow charts. Screens wait for real data.

04

One design language

One tab, one dialog, one stat card, one product picker.

05

Restrained colour

Neutral surfaces. Colour only when it carries meaning.

06

Mobile-native

The inbox folds into list, then thread, on a phone.

06

Structure and direction

Onboarding

Four steps, Shopify first. The right side sells while you sign up: a real DM thread ending in an order.

Copilot home

Opens on what Pipr earned today, one box to ask anything, and ideas ranked by money: win back 1,386 lapsed buyers, worth ₹3.3L.

Answers as cards, not paragraphs

Ask "check my products for Google" and get a score, the products that need it most and one Fix button. You check the first batch, and every change can be undone.

Content: every post, and what it sold

Every Instagram and Facebook post with its tagged products, links sent and AI credits used. Tag a post, and Pipr sends those products to everyone who comments or DMs about it.

Tag products

Pick from Shopify, or describe a product in plain words when it isn't listed yet.

Help desk inbox

Hundreds of chats grouped into a handful of reasons, so a human only sees what needs one.

Fix once, fix everywhere

Correct any reply in place. Pipr shows how many chats the fix would change.

Train like a new hire

Questions Pipr couldn't answer surface on their own. Answer once, and it knows.

WhatsApp campaigns

COD confirmation, cart recovery and order updates as ready templates, with live delivery and read numbers.

08

Key decisions

01

The agent is the home screen

Founders don't want another dashboard. They ask, Pipr answers, and the answer is a card they can act on.

02

Every idea has a price tag

Suggestions show the money at stake and the credits they cost, so the yes is easy.

03

Grouped, not queued

Hundreds of chats become a handful of reasons.

04

Teach, don't configure

No flow builders. Correct a reply and Pipr learns it everywhere.
