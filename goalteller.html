01

Context

GoalTeller is a financial planning platform that helps families track, analyse and manage wealth spread across many managers. I did their initial UI/UX years ago. In 2026 I audited the web product screen by screen and designed the mobile app.

02

The problem

Families hold money with five or six managers. Each sends its own report. Nobody sees the whole picture, knows when the investment policy is breached, or which manager is actually beating the benchmark.

03

Research

How

Screen-by-screen audit of the web productEvery popup, state and edge case cataloguedFounder feedback loops

What we learned

Insight 01

Breaches hide in totals

A portfolio can look fine while one asset class is far over its limit.

Insight 02

Managers can't be compared

Every report has its own format and benchmark.

Insight 03

Metrics need meaning

XIRR and expense ratio mean little without context.

04

Who it's for

T

Manages money for the whole family across 5+ managers. Wants one honest view.

T

The busy professional

Checks in monthly. Wants to know if anything needs action.

05

The design

Home

Net worth, XIRR against benchmark, what needs attention, allocation, managers and insights. One scroll.

Breach, compare, ask

Policy breaches with the exact gap, managers ranked in one table, and an AI that explains the numbers.

06

Key decisions

01

What's breaking the policy shows above the charts.

02

03
