Context

HROne automates payroll, attendance, recruitment, performance and engagement for 2,000+ organisations across 20+ industries. I redesigned the web and mobile product end to end.

02

The problem

The product worked, but as it scaled the experience didn't. HR managers navigated dense screens that needed training, and mobile was an afterthought.

03

Research

How

HR managers observed through a working dayAudit of every module and flowEmployee mobile usage

What we learned

Insight 01

The day, not the database

Menus mirrored tables, not tasks.

Insight 02

Mobile is for employees

Attendance, leave and pay are phone jobs.

Insight 03

Consistency is speed

Every module had its own patterns.

04

Who it's for

H

HR manager

Runs payroll and approvals all day.

E

Employee

Checks in, applies for leave and views pay on the phone.

05

The design

Home

A home built around the day: attendance, celebrations, approvals, calendar.

System and mobile

A teal system with a full icon set. Pay details made glanceable on mobile.

06

Key decisions

01

03
