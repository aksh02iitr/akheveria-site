3. What a designer catches

Nothing here is broken. It just doesn’t do the job. These are the notes I’d leave on it.

  1. A headline thousands of sites share.“Product designer who ships” says nothing a buyer will remember tomorrow.
  2. An illustration instead of the work.The biggest space on the page shows a drawing, not a real screen.
  3. Facts out of date.“Open for January” was true once. AI kept it because nobody told it otherwise.
  4. Numbers without the story.Five big numbers, no project to click behind any of them.
  1. Placeholder thumbnails.Coloured bars stand in for products that have real, shipped screens.
  2. Seven filters before one project.The buyer is asked to sort before they’re given a reason to care.
